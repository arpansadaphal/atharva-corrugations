## 6. Photography brief

### 6.1 Principles
Real, documentary, true colour. Operators and inspectors in PPE in at least 30% of plant frames. No customer branding visible unless cleared. No staged handshakes, no posed rows. Light with the plant's own overhead lighting plus one soft fill; no theatrical spotlights, smoke or lens flare.

**Tier 3:** neutral-background product portraits per format, dark or white backdrop consistent across the set.

### 6.3 Technical and handling rules
- Camera RAW or maximum-quality JPEG; ≥3000 px on the long edge; keep originals.
- White balance neutral to slightly warm; no filters or presets.
- Name files `acpl-[subject]-[view]-[nn].jpg`; keep a log of subject, date, who approved.
- **Employee consent:** a short written consent from every identifiable person (Technical §11).
- **Confidentiality:** remove or mask customer names, logos and product details on boxes and screens unless cleared in writing.
- **Safety:** shoot only where PPE rules are met; no photographs that show unsafe practice.

### 6.4 If a photographer is not possible
The dieline-led hero (Hero A) and our own drawings carry the site. P-04 and P-05 can be shot on a phone in an hour with a plain backdrop and daylight. Sections that depend on plant photography are omitted until photographs exist.

## 10. Copywriting plan

### 10.1 Process
We draft; a named client-side technical reviewer checks flute, strength and process wording (SD-10); the owner approves; each claim receives a registry ID; nothing ships unreviewed.

### 10.2 Style
- Voice: experienced manufacturing partner. Specific, operational, plain.
- British/Indian English spelling (customised, colour). Sentence-case headings.
- Units: millimetres for dimensions, kilograms for weight, "GSM" spelled out on first use.
- Phone format `+91 99603 78111`; dates `12 Oct 2026`; no digit grouping surprises (avoid lakhs/crore figures entirely).
- Terms to define on first use: ply, flute, GSM, internal dimensions, dieline. Terms to confirm with the client before use: HRSC, "pasting", "offset-printed lamination", "core pipes", BCB/CA/AA/AAA/ACA.
- **Banned:** world-class, industry-leading, cutting-edge, revolutionary, next-generation, global presence, "trusted partner", "one-stop", "state-of-the-art", any superlative without proof.
- Every sentence containing a number, a capability or a certification maps to a registry entry.

### 10.3 Ready microcopy for the quote flow

**Quote page introduction.** "Tell us what you pack, the size and the quantity. We reply within 2 working days, Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 6 pm. In a hurry? Message us on WhatsApp."

**Field help.** What are you packing: "For example: compressor housing, about 40 kg." Internal size: "Measure inside the box, in millimetres: length × width × height." Quantity: "Add more than one quantity if you want to compare prices." Required-by date: "The date you need the boxes in hand."

**Success.** "Thank you. Your reference is {reference}. We have emailed you a copy. Our team will reply within 2 working days. Have a drawing, dieline or photograph? WhatsApp it to +91 99603 78111 with your reference, or reply to our email with the file attached."

**Error.** "We could not send your enquiry. Your details are still on this page. Please try again, or message us on WhatsApp at +91 99603 78111, or call."

**Auto-reply subject.** "We received your enquiry {reference} · Atharva Corrugations".
**Auto-reply body.** Reference; the details submitted; "We reply within 2 working days (Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 6 pm)"; "To send a drawing, reply to this email with the file attached or message us on WhatsApp"; contact line; plant address as per the verified address string.

**WhatsApp template.** "Hello Atharva Corrugations, I would like a quote for {family or page}. Ref: {reference or source token}."
